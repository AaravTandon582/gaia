What each method contributes

Trend and screeningHamed–Rao Mann–Kendall on raw monthly data, Sen slope, and detrended residual screening.
Regime changesBOCPD on standardized deseasonalized observations. Candidate boundaries are heuristic.
PCMCIClimatology removal, detrending, standardization, ParCorr at lags 1–6, BH q < 0.05.
Transfer entropyKSG estimator, k = 5, history = 2, 200 circular-shift surrogates on retained links.
LSTM and waveletsMultivariate reconstruction error and a separate view of variation across timescales.
Screening, lag tests, and reconstruction diagnostics answer different questions.
Sources: backend/analysis/README.md, backend/agents/README.md, backend/ml/README.md. The BOCPD implementation uses a Normal–Inverse–Gamma model and Student-t prediction, hazard lambda 200, minimum run length 5, and heuristic shorter-run posterior threshold 0.5. Default scouts now expose candidate dates. They do not use those dates to change variable screening or PCMCI selection. The wavelet view has edge artifacts and does not provide a calibrated power spectral density.

Technical backup / 3

Questions behind the demo

Does a link establish causation?

No. It is a conditional statistical association. Unmeasured drivers, selection, and product mismatch remain possible.

Why do both regions show 19 flags?

The same combined model scores all 12 features. The display does not attribute those flags to one region.

What happens when a scan stops?

The socket closes, request cancellation reaches workers, and the interface discards late results.

What remains unvalidated?

Independent anomaly accuracy, complete physical closure, and operation at a public deployment URL.

The browser deck and embedded demo work offline. The live map needs internet access for external tiles.
